## Context: what "Ramp" is (from spec #32)

A **Ramp** is an optional rule, off by default, that steps phase durations from the active
Preset's values toward a target as cycles complete. Two ramps are planned; T1 ships only
**Wind Down** — Exhale lengthens by 1s every 2 completed cycles, up to +4s, never past 15s.
Hold and Rest never change. Like a Session Goal, the Ramp selected at Start applies for the
whole Session; changing it mid-Session takes effect on the next Session.
