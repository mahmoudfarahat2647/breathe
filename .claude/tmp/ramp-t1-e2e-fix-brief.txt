## Delta task — fix one parity e2e selector collision introduced by the Ramp picker

The Ramp T1 work added a **RampPicker** inside the "Show advanced options" panel with two
chips: "Off" and "Wind down". The "Off" chip text now collides with the Sound toggle's
visible "Off" word in tests that use an unscoped `getByText("Off")` once the advanced panel
is open.

`pnpm vitest run` is already green (a `within(...)` scope was added to
`src/test/presentation/breathe-app.test.tsx`). One Playwright test still fails:

    e2e/parity.spec.ts:66
    > await expect(page.getByText("Off")).toBeVisible();
    strict mode violation: getByText('Off') resolved to 2 elements
      1) <span>Off</span>            (the Sound toggle word)
      2) <button aria-pressed="true">Off</button>  (the new Ramp chip)

### Change — `e2e/parity.spec.ts` only

At line 66, scope the assertion to the Sound toggle so it targets the toggle's visible word,
not the Ramp chip. The Sound word is a `<span>` inside `<span class="mv-sound-toggle">`.
Change:

    await expect(page.getByText("Off")).toBeVisible();

to:

    await expect(
      page.locator(".mv-sound-toggle").getByText("Off"),
    ).toBeVisible();

That is the whole change. Do not touch any other line, any other file, the RampPicker, or
`src/`. The intent of the assertion (parity row "Sound switch opt-in (default off) with
visible On/Off word") is preserved and made more precise.
